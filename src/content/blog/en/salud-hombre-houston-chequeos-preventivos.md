# Men's Health in Houston: Preventive Checkups That Can Save Your Life

Many men only see a doctor when something already hurts. The problem is that the most dangerous diseases — high blood pressure, diabetes, high cholesterol and prostate problems — progress for years **without any symptoms**. By the time warning signs appear, the damage is often already done.

At **Clínica Hispana Familiar 529** we want to change that story. A preventive checkup takes less than an hour, requires no appointment, and can catch conditions early that, if ignored, end in a heart attack, a stroke or worse.

## Why Should Men Get Regular Checkups?

The statistics are clear: men visit the doctor far less often than women, so their conditions are diagnosed later. An annual checkup allows you to:

- **Detect hypertension, diabetes and high cholesterol** before they damage your heart, kidneys or eyesight
- **Monitor prostate health** with a simple blood test (PSA)
- **Measure your testosterone level**, which affects energy, mood and sexual health
- **Prevent heart attacks and strokes** by controlling risk factors in time
- **Compare your results year over year** to spot changes before they become problems

## Our Men's Health Services

At our clinic in northwest Houston we offer a complete evaluation designed for men. Learn more about our [men's health exams: PSA and testosterone](/services/salud-hombre).

### General Men's Checkup

A complete evaluation with vital signs: blood pressure, weight, glucose and a physical exam. It's the baseline for knowing your real state of health.

### Prostate Exam (PSA)

The prostate-specific antigen (PSA) is a simple blood test that helps monitor prostate health. It's especially important after age 40-50 or if you have a family history of prostate cancer.

### Male Hormone Profile

Low testosterone causes fatigue, low mood, weight gain and sexual problems. With a blood test we measure your level and explain the results in Spanish or English.

### Lab Work

We have our own [clinical laboratory](/services/examenes-sangre): glucose, cholesterol, triglycerides, kidney and liver function, complete blood count and more — no need to go anywhere else.

### Electrocardiogram

An [electrocardiogram (EKG)](/services/electrocardiograma) evaluates your heart's rhythm and electrical activity in minutes. It's key if you have high blood pressure, chest pain or a history of heart problems.

## Warning Signs: Don't Ignore Them

Come to the clinic as soon as possible if you experience:

- **Chest pain or pressure**, especially with exertion
- Excessive thirst and frequent urination (possible diabetes)
- Difficulty, burning or dribbling when urinating
- Persistent fatigue that doesn't improve with rest
- Frequent headaches (possible high blood pressure)
- Changes in sexual desire or performance
- Lumps or changes in the testicles

## Practical Tips for Taking Care of Your Health

1. **Get a checkup every year**, even if you feel fine
2. **Know your numbers**: blood pressure, glucose and cholesterol
3. **Move 30 minutes a day** — walking counts
4. **Cut back on sodas, alcohol and fried food**
5. **Sleep at least 7 hours** on a regular schedule
6. **Don't wait for symptoms** to see a doctor
